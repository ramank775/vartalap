Information architecture

Vartalap keeps three things apart. Chats is a live inbox: a row exists there only once a conversation has at least one message, so an untouched group or a contact you have never written to is never in the list. Contacts and Groups are directories of who and what you can write to; in 3.0 they are the two tabs of the New chat picker, reached from the Chats FAB, which is why no bottom navigation bar is needed. Deleting a chat is a local inbox action: the row leaves Chats and the local history is erased, but membership is untouched and the group is still listed under Groups, ready to be reopened from the picker. Leaving a group is a separate, deliberate, server-side action that lives at the bottom of group info and removes the group from Groups as well. Identity follows one rule everywhere another person appears: their name from your address book if you hold their number, otherwise their @username. No other user's phone number is ever rendered, and your own is visible only to you, on your own profile.

  • 01Chats, Contacts, Groups are three things. A group with no messages lives in Groups only.
  • 02Delete chat ≠ Leave group. Delete chat is local: row + history. Leave group is a server op in group info.
  • 03New chat opens a picker with Contacts and Groups tabs; tapping a group opens its chat, which joins Chats once a message exists.
  • 043.1 splits Chats into Personal and Other. The 3.0 screen already carries the segment slot, collapsed to nothing.
  • 05@username is required and public; the phone number is private. Every account claims a handle at sign-up; people appear as a saved contact name or as @handle, never as a number.
  • 06Avatar: photo when set, else initials on a stable per-id colour. Users and groups both carry a photo; the initials tile is the fallback, not the default.

Open questions for the owner

Contacts and Groups as destinations. They are the two tabs of the New chat picker, not a bottom navigation bar — the brief mandates the picker and a FAB, and a nav bar would put three tabs in front of an inbox that is the only screen most sessions need. If Contacts and Groups should be reachable without going through “New chat”, the cheapest change is retitling that screen to “Contacts & Groups” and adding a second entry point from the overflow menu; no frame changes.
Delete chat on a 1:1. Same local semantics, but there is no membership to keep, so the sheet's note reads “The contact stays in Contacts” instead of the group wording.
Muted chats and the unread badge. A muted chat still counts unread messages; the badge just loses its colour (outline grey). The alternative — hiding the count — loses information the user asked to keep receiving.
Two label forms, never three. A row reads as an address-book name or as @handle; both appear across the frames on purpose. Because the handle is required at sign-up there is no third, transitional form — no phone number is rendered for another user anywhere, which also means the unsaved-sender row needs no extra layout.
Where 3.1's classifier lives. The Personal / Other split is drawn as a client-side filter over one table, so 3.0 rows need no new column beyond a nullable bucket the 3.1 classifier fills. Frame b assumes that.
